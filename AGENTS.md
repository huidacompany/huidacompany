# AGENTS.md

## Mission
Rewrite and optimize product detail pages for a laboratory glassware website so they are clearer, more useful for search engines, and more persuasive for inquiry conversion.

The website is not a general retail store. Product pages should support real purchasing decisions, product evaluation, and supplier screening.

---

## Brand Voice

Write in a tone that is:

- professional
- clear
- friendly
- confident
- helpful
- technically responsible

The writing should sound like an experienced manufacturer or supplier speaking directly to a serious buyer, not like an aggressive salesperson and not like a generic AI article.

### Tone rules
- Use simple, natural English.
- Keep sentences clear and readable.
- Sound knowledgeable without sounding stiff.
- Be warm and approachable, but not casual.
- Be persuasive through clarity, not hype.
- Focus on real product value, application fit, and purchasing convenience.
- Avoid robotic repetition and keyword stuffing.
- Avoid exaggerated claims unless they are verified.
- Avoid empty phrases such as "best quality", "perfect solution", "premium experience", or "world-class" unless supported by context.

### Tone example
Preferred:
- Designed for routine laboratory use
- Suitable for liquid handling and sample preparation
- Available in multiple sizes for different testing workflows
- Contact us for specifications, packaging details, or a quotation

Avoid:
- This is the best product on the market
- You must buy now
- Unbeatable quality and price
- Revolutionary laboratory product

## Core Writing Principles

Every product page must be:

- unique
- accurate
- easy to scan
- suitable for SEO
- suitable for inquiry conversion
- useful for real procurement decisions

### Always do
- Keep all technical facts consistent with the source product data.
- Make each SKU page meaningfully distinct from similar products.
- Explain what the product is, what it is used for, and who it may suit.
- Use headings that help both readers and search engines.
- Add practical application language when relevant.
- Include a natural inquiry prompt.
- Use concrete wording instead of vague praise.
- Make the page useful for both first-time visitors and experienced buyers.

### Never do
- Invent specifications, certifications, materials, or compliance details.
- Copy the same paragraph structure across too many SKU pages without variation.
- Use vague filler content only to increase word count.
- Overuse the target keyword unnaturally.
- Write content that sounds like a blog post when the page is a product page.
- Write customer-facing copy that explicitly says "B2B", "B-end", or similar terms.

---

## SEO Rules

### Titles
- Product titles must be accurate, descriptive, and aligned with the actual product.
- Keep SEO titles clear and readable.
- Prefer keyword-first structure when natural.
- Do not force the same title pattern onto every product.

### Meta descriptions
- Write for click-through, not just ranking.
- Keep them clear, specific, and natural.
- Mention the product type, use case, and inquiry intent when appropriate.
- Avoid duplicate meta descriptions across similar pages.

### Headings
- One clear H1 only.
- Use H2 and H3 to break up sections logically.
- Avoid heading fluff such as "Why This Product Is Amazing".

### Keyword usage
- Use the primary keyword naturally in:
  - H1
  - first paragraph
  - one or more subheadings where relevant
  - image alt suggestions
  - meta title or meta description where natural
- Include close variants and related terms naturally.
- Do not force exact-match repetition.

### Internal linking
- Suggest 3 to 5 relevant internal links where possible.
- Prefer related product families, buying guides, and application articles.
- Use natural anchor text.

## Conversion Rules

Every product page should help move the visitor toward inquiry.

### CTA style
Use natural inquiry language such as:
- Contact us for a quote
- Send us your inquiry
- Ask for specifications or pricing
- Get in touch for packaging details or bulk order information
- Contact our team for product recommendations

Avoid:
- Buy now
- Order today before it's too late
- Best deal guaranteed

---

## Accuracy Rules

Only use claims that are supported by available product information.

If the source data does not clearly confirm something, do not state it as fact.

Examples:
- Do not claim borosilicate glass unless confirmed.
- Do not claim Class A accuracy unless confirmed.
- Do not claim autoclavable unless confirmed.
- Do not claim OEM support on a specific product unless generally valid for the business.

When data is incomplete:
- keep the wording general
- avoid hard claims
- leave room for inquiry

---

## Style Rules for Similar SKU Pages

When many products are in the same family:
- vary the opening paragraph
- vary sentence rhythm
- vary feature emphasis
- vary application phrasing
- vary CTA wording slightly
- do not reuse nearly identical FAQ blocks unless necessary

Each page should feel individually written, even when the structure is shared.

---

## Output Rules

When rewriting a product page, output in this exact order:

1. H1
2. SEO Title
3. Meta Description
4. Short Description
5. Long Description
6. Key Features
7. Specifications Section Copy
8. Applications
9. FAQ
10. CTA
11. Internal Link Suggestions
12. Image Alt Text Suggestions

Do not publish automatically.
Do not alter product data that was not explicitly provided.
