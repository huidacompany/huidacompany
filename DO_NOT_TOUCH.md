# DO_NOT_TOUCH.md

## Purpose
These items must not be changed automatically during product page rewriting or SEO optimization work.

---

## Product Data Restrictions

Do not change:
- price
- sale price
- SKU
- stock quantity
- stock status
- shipping weight
- shipping dimensions
- product attributes used for backend filtering
- product category assignments
- product tags unless explicitly requested
- product variations
- product identifiers
- packaging quantity values unless explicitly confirmed
- materials, tolerances, or technical specifications unless explicitly confirmed

---

## Media Restrictions

Do not change:
- featured image
- gallery images
- image filenames
- product videos
- downloadable files
- certificates
- PDFs
- image cropping or image replacement

Only provide image alt text suggestions when requested by workflow output.

---

## Publishing Restrictions

Do not:
- publish automatically
- update live pages automatically
- schedule publication automatically
- change post status to published
- overwrite approved content without explicit instruction

Preferred workflow:
- draft
- review
- manual approval
- manual publish

---

## Site Structure Restrictions

Do not change:
- navigation menus
- breadcrumbs settings
- permalinks
- URL slug structure rules
- redirects
- canonical rules
- robots.txt
- sitemap settings
- theme-wide layout settings
- header
- footer
- sidebar logic
- search configuration
- category page templates
- archive page templates

---

## Plugin and SEO Settings Restrictions

Do not change:
- Rank Math global settings
- title template settings
- schema defaults
- redirection settings
- analytics settings
- verification codes
- indexing rules
- noindex settings
- Open Graph defaults
- site-wide metadata settings

You may suggest SEO title and meta description copy, but do not directly alter plugin-wide configuration unless explicitly instructed.

---

## Commerce and Functional Restrictions

Do not change:
- checkout settings
- payment settings
- inquiry form behavior
- cart settings
- tax settings
- currency settings
- coupon settings
- user account settings
- email templates
- shipping methods

---

## Design Restrictions

Do not change:
- Blocksy global design settings
- typography system
- color system
- spacing system
- button styles
- product page layout structure outside approved content areas
- product tabs configuration unless explicitly requested

Content rewriting is allowed.
Layout redesign is not allowed unless explicitly requested.

---

## Claim Restrictions

Do not add or modify claims related to:
- certification
- regulatory compliance
- test reports
- sterility
- temperature resistance
- chemical resistance
- measurement class
- tolerance class
- material grade
- country-specific compliance

Unless the claim is clearly supported by the product source data.

---

## Safety Rule

When information is missing, uncertain, or ambiguous:
- do not guess
- do not fill gaps with generic claims
- do not infer technical details as facts
- keep the wording general
- leave room for inquiry

---

## Priority Rule

If there is a conflict between better marketing language and source accuracy, accuracy wins.

If there is a conflict between SEO expansion and technical truth, technical truth wins.

If there is a conflict between automation speed and review safety, review safety wins.
