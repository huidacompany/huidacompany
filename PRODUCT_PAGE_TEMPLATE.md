# PRODUCT_PAGE_TEMPLATE.md

## Purpose
This template defines the required structure for every laboratory glassware product page rewrite.

The goal is to make product pages:
- clear
- unique
- SEO-friendly
- conversion-oriented
- suitable for serious procurement and product evaluation

---

## Required Output Structure

### 1. H1
- Use the real product name
- Keep it accurate and specific
- Match the actual product type, size, or format where relevant

---

### 2. SEO Title
Requirements:
- clear and readable
- aligned with the product name
- include the primary keyword naturally
- suitable for search results
- avoid duplication across similar products

Preferred examples:
- Volumetric Flask with Ground Glass Stopper
- Glass Measuring Cylinder with Hexagonal Base
- Borosilicate Glass Beaker for Laboratory Use

---

### 3. Meta Description
Requirements:
- natural and click-worthy
- around normal search snippet length
- mention product type and real use context
- invite inquiry where appropriate
- avoid generic wording

Preferred style:
- Source laboratory glassware from a reliable manufacturer. Explore sizes, specifications, and application details, and contact us for a quote.

---

### 4. Short Description
Requirements:
- 1 short paragraph
- clear product summary
- visible value for distributors, laboratories, universities, research institutions, or procurement teams
- suitable for top-of-page placement

What it should do:
- explain what the product is
- briefly state what it is used for
- show one or two practical advantages

---

### 5. Long Description
Requirements:
- 2 to 5 paragraphs depending on product complexity
- natural flow
- written for real buyers and users
- not repetitive

Suggested structure:
- paragraph 1: what the product is and where it fits
- paragraph 2: material, design, or usability features
- paragraph 3: common use scenarios
- paragraph 4: supply, packaging, or buyer-oriented reassurance if relevant

---

### 6. Key Features
Requirements:
- 4 to 6 bullet points
- concise
- specific
- no fluff

Possible feature angles:
- material
- graduation clarity
- thermal suitability
- chemical resistance
- stable base or practical shape
- multiple capacities
- laboratory suitability
- packaging support
- OEM/private label support if appropriate

---

### 7. Specifications Section Copy
Requirements:
- introduce the specifications clearly
- support table-based product data
- do not invent numbers

Suggested intro line:
- Please refer to the specification table below for available sizes, dimensions, capacity options, and other product details.

If the page already has an attribute table:
- keep this section short
- use it to guide the reader into the table

---

### 8. Applications
Requirements:
- 1 short intro paragraph
- 4 to 6 practical bullet points or short paragraphs
- must fit the actual product type

Possible application contexts:
- analytical laboratories
- chemistry laboratories
- research institutions
- educational laboratories
- pharmaceutical laboratories
- industrial testing environments
- quality control laboratories

Avoid generic application lists that do not match the product.

---

### 9. FAQ
Requirements:
- 4 to 6 questions
- concise answers
- practical buyer-focused questions
- avoid repeating the same FAQ on every page without adaptation

Good FAQ topics:
- available sizes
- packaging
- material options
- OEM/private label
- quotation process
- suitable use scenarios
- lead time inquiry
- related product selection

Avoid:
- questions that require unverified claims
- meaningless filler questions

---

### 10. Inquiry CTA
Requirements:
- 1 short paragraph
- natural and direct
- not pushy

Preferred CTA styles:
- Contact us for a quote, product details, or packaging information.
- Get in touch with our team for specifications, availability, or custom requirements.
- Send us your inquiry if you need product recommendations or bulk order support.

---

### 11. Internal Link Suggestions
Requirements:
- 3 to 5 suggestions
- relevant only
- use natural anchor text

Suggested link targets:
- closely related product pages
- product family pages
- application guides
- buying guides
- laboratory glassware category pages

---

### 12. Image Alt Text Suggestions
Requirements:
- descriptive
- product-specific
- natural
- not stuffed

Good format examples:
- glass volumetric flask with stopper
- graduated measuring cylinder laboratory glassware
- borosilicate glass beaker for chemical laboratory use

---

## Writing Length Guidance

### Short products
Use shorter copy for:
- simple glassware items with limited technical difference

### Technical or high-value products
Use more developed copy for:
- volumetric glassware
- distillation glassware
- extraction glassware
- condenser systems
- burettes
- complex assembled glassware

---

## Uniqueness Rules

For similar product pages:
- vary paragraph openings
- vary feature order
- vary the emphasis
- vary CTA phrasing
- vary FAQ phrasing
- avoid near-duplicate intros and conclusions

The structure may stay consistent, but the wording must not feel mass-produced.

---

## Final Validation Checklist

Before final output, confirm:
- the product type is correct
- the wording matches the available specifications
- the page has one clear H1
- the meta description is unique enough
- the page includes applications
- the page includes FAQ
- the CTA feels natural
- the internal links are relevant
- the content does not explicitly say B2B or B-end
- the page is useful for distributors, laboratories, procurement teams, universities, and research institutions without naming them all at once unless needed
